Modern Greek Spelling Rules

Every spelling pattern a Glossomathia student needs, from the first Greek letters at A1 through the literary forms at C2. Each level stands on its own: find yours, master those rules, then move up.

A1A2B1B2C1C2

How to use this guide

  • Work through your level first, then read higher levels as a preview.
  • The cheat sheet at the end applies to every level. Bookmark it.
  • Say every Greek example aloud. Spelling and sound are deeply linked in Modern Greek.

A note on the system

Modern Greek uses the monotonic system: one accent mark, the tonos ΄, on the stressed syllable of every word with two or more syllables. Greek spelling is largely phonemic. Once you know the rules, you can spell almost anything you can say.

A1

Foundation

The Greek alphabet, basic sounds, the accent, and the first digraphs

Rule 1

σ vs ς: two forms of sigma

The letter sigma has two forms. Use σ everywhere except at the very end of a word, where you use ς.

PositionLetterExample
Inside a wordσσπίτι, ιστορία
End of a wordςκαλημέρας, φίλος
Rule 2

The accent (tonos)

Every word with two or more syllables carries exactly one accent on the stressed syllable. Capital letters carry no accent.

WordMeaningStressed syllable
σπίτιhouseσπίτι
ημέραdayημέρα
τραπέζιtableτραπέζι

A1 Practice

1. Final σ or ς?

Add the correct sigma to complete each word.

  1. καλημέρα_ (good morning)
  2. _πίτι (house)
  3. φίλο_ (friend)
  4. ι_τορία (history)
Answers
καλημέρας · σπίτι · φίλος · ιστορία

2. Where does the accent go?

Add one accent to each word. Say it aloud first.

  1. ημερα (day)
  2. βιβλιο (book)
  3. φιλος (friend)
  4. τραπεζι (table)
Answers
ημέρα · βιβλίο · φίλος · τραπέζι

3. Digraph match

Match each digraph to its sound: μπ · ντ · αυ · οι

Answers
μπ = /b/ · ντ = /d/ · αυ = /af/ or /av/ · οι = /ee/

A2 Practice

1. Which /ee/ spelling?

MeaningOptions
dayημέρα / ιμέρα / ειμέρα
houseσπίτι / σπήτι / σπείτι
familyοικογένεια / ικογένεια

Answers

2. ο or ω?

At A2, the default is ο.

  1. λόγ_ς (word)
  2. οδ_ς (road)
  3. καλ_ (neuter adjective: good)
Answers
λόγος · οδός · καλό. All use ο at A2.

3. What gender?

Look at the article and write M, F or N.

  1. η ζώνη
  2. ο δάσκαλος
  3. το σχολείο
  4. οι μαθητές (plural: which genders could this be?)
Answers
1. F · 2. M · 3. N · 4. M or F (οι is the plural article for both)

Rule 7

Verbs always end in -ω, never -ο

The first person singular present tense of every verb ends in -ω. Never use ο here.

B1 Practice

1. Fix the verb ending

Each verb has a spelling mistake. Correct it.

  1. εγώ γράφο
  2. εγώ τρώγο
  3. εγώ αγαπο
  4. εγώ πηγαίνο
Answers
γράφω · τρώω · αγαπώ · πηγαίνω

2. Write the third person

Change from εγώ to αυτός/ή/ό.

  1. γράφω
  2. τρώω
  3. αγαπώ
Answers
γράφει · τρώει · αγαπά (contract verb)

3. ο or ω?

  1. γράφ_ (I write)
  2. τ_ρα (now)
  3. καλ_ς (well, adverb)
  4. λόγ_ς (word, noun)
Answers
γράφω (verb) · τώρα (fixed form) · καλώς (adverb) · λόγος (noun)

4. Make the adjective agree

Change καλός to match each noun.

  1. η ζώνη: καλ_
  2. το σπίτι: καλ_
  3. οι φίλοι: καλ_
Answers
καλή · καλό · καλοί
B2

Upper Intermediate

Feminine -η vs -α, masculine -ης endings, neuter -υ exceptions, adverbs in -ως

Rule 11

Feminine nouns: -η is default, -α after ε, ι, λ, ν, ρ

Feminine nouns take -η by default. They take -α when the preceding letter is ε, ι, λ, ν or ρ.

EndingPreceding letterExample
-ηDefaultη ζώνη, η φωνή
-αAfter ε, ι, λ, ν, ρη θάλασσα (λ), η χώρα (ρ), η γιαγιά (ι)
Rule 12

Masculine nouns in -ης use η

WordMeaning
ο μαθητήςstudent
ο πολίτηςcitizen
ο πελάτηςcustomer

Always η here, never ι or ει.

B2 Practice

1. η or α?

IncompleteMeaning
η ζών_belt
η θάλασσ_sea
η χώρ_country
η γιαγι_grandma
η φων_voice
Answers
ζώνη (default) · θάλασσα (after λ) · χώρα (after ρ) · γιαγιά (after ι) · φωνή (default)

2. Spot the fake -υ word

Five are real neuter -υ nouns. One is invented. Cross out the fake.

βράδυ · δίχτυ · σπίτυ · δάκρυ · δόρυ · γόνυ

Answer
σπίτυ is not a real word. The correct form is σπίτι (neuter -ι).

3. Form the adverb

Add -ως to each adjective.

  1. καλός
  2. αργός
  3. σωστός
Answers

Rule 16

Double consonants

Modern Greek has very few genuine double consonants. They appear in:

  • Compounds where prefix and root meet: συν- + ναύτης = συνναύτης
  • Learned words: θάλασσα, γραμματική, αλλά
Rule 17

Tricky homophones at advanced level

PairDifference
αλλά / άλλααλλά = but · άλλα = other things (neut. pl.)
αυτός / αυτώςαυτός = he, himself · αυτώς = in person
παρά / παρα-preposition vs. prefix: one word vs. compound
C1 · C2 Practice

1. Choose the right homophone

  1. The answer is _____ right. (σωστός / σωστώς)
  2. He came _____ person. (αυτός / αυτώς)
  3. _____ I don't agree. (αλλά / άλλα)
Answers
σωστώς (adverb) · αυτώς (in person) · αλλά (but)

2. Double consonant or not?

  1. θάλασα / θάλασσα
  2. αλα / αλλά
  3. γραματική / γραμματική
  4. καλος / καλλος
Answers
θάλασσα · αλλά · γραμματική · καλός (single)

3. Prefix + root compounds

  1. συν- + ναύτης
  2. κατα- + γράφω
Answers
συνναύτης (double νν) · καταγράφω (single γ, no change)
Every level

Master Cheat Sheet

These rules apply at every level and fix the most common spelling mistakes.

The 5 rules never to forget

  1. σ vs ς. ς only at the end of a word; σ everywhere else.
  2. Verbs end in -ω. Never -ο in first person singular present.
  3. Third person is -ει. γράφει, not γράφε or γράφη.
  4. Every word gets one accent. Capital letters get none.
  5. Feminine default is -η; after ε, ι, λ, ν, ρ it is -α. γιαγιά, θάλασσα, χώρα.
