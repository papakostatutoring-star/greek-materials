Χαμένοι στην Παλιά Πόλη

You are on holiday in Κέρκυρα with Ελένη. You took a wrong turn in the old town and lost your group. Your boat ticket is in a little lockbox with a 4 digit code.

Pass four places in the old town. Each one gives you one digit. Then open the box and catch the boat!
